Principal Investigator

实验室负责人

Zhubing Shi · 史竹兵

特聘研究员 · 博士生导师

Principal Investigator, School of Life Sciences, Westlake University

史竹兵博士，西湖大学生命科学学院特聘研究员、博士生导师。主要从事结构生物学研究， 运用冷冻电镜（cryo-EM）、X射线晶体学、生物化学及生物物理学方法， 研究基因转录调控和染色质三维组织中的关键大分子复合体的结构与功能。 研究工作聚焦于 cohesin、Mediator、转录因子等染色质相关蛋白复合体的组装机制及其在基因表达调控和 基因组三维折叠中的作用。

Research Interests

研究方向

🔬

3D Genome Organization

Structural and functional studies of cohesin, CTCF, and NIPBL-MAU2 complexes in mediating chromatin loop extrusion and three-dimensional genome folding.

🧬

Transcription Regulation

Cryo-EM studies of Mediator complex, CDK8 module, and their interactions with transcription factors and the basal transcription machinery.

💻

Integrative Structural Biology

Combining cryo-EM, X-ray crystallography, crosslinking mass spectrometry, and computational modeling to study dynamic macromolecular assemblies.

🧪

Drug Discovery

Structure-based drug design targeting chromatin-modifying complexes and transcriptional regulators implicated in cancer and developmental diseases.
